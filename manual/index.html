Business Owner Guide

Master Administrator
1

Account Registration & Profile Setup

Creating your business account and setting up core identity

When registering for BMSTz as a Business Owner (BSO), you receive complete administrative control over your branches, financial reports, pricing plans, and staff permissions.

Step-by-Step Account Setup:

  1. Navigate to the login screen and click Create new account.
  2. Select Business Owner (BSO) as your registration role type.
  3. Enter your business name, email address, full name, and create a secure password.
  4. Upon logging in, navigate to Settings → Business Profile to upload your official business logo, set your primary currency (TZS / USD), and configure tax rules.
2

Branch Creation & Staff Management

Adding new physical branches and assigning Branch Managers (BR)

BMSTz allows you to operate multiple branch locations seamlessly under a unified business account.

Creating a New Branch

Navigate to Branches → Add Branch. Enter the branch location name, physical address, and contact line. The branch will immediately appear on your master overview.

Assigning Managers (BR)

Go to Staff → Add Manager. Assign the manager to their target branch and provide login credentials. Managers operate under the system role prefix BR.

3

Branch Security & Administrative Lockout System

Configuring branch operational lockouts and monitoring audit violations

Enforce strict operational policies across your branches to protect inventory, verify cash floats, and block unauthorized price alterations.

Setting Branch Security Preferences:

  • Open Branches → Branch Preferences for any branch location.
  • Require Cash Drawer Opening: Mandate that managers record an opening float before sales recording is unlocked.
  • Block Price Overrides: Disallow managers from manually altering product selling prices at checkout.
  • Block Stock Adjustments: Require BSO authorization before stock quantities can be adjusted.
  • Security & Lockout Manager: Any unauthorized attempt by a Branch Manager to bypass locked preferences triggers a Security Violation Alert in your audit feed for instant review.

System Role Prefix: BR

Branch Manager Guide

Branch Operations
1

Daily Shift Operations & Cash Drawer Floating

Opening shifts, recording starting cash float, and shift closure

As a Branch Manager (BR), your daily workflow starts with opening a daily cash shift.

Shift Execution Procedure:

  1. Log in using your manager credentials assigned by your Business Owner (BSO).
  2. Go to Cash Drawer → Open Shift. Enter your starting cash float amount.
  3. During the day, all sales, branch expenses, and loan collections update your drawer balance in real-time.
  4. At the end of your working shift, click Close Shift, input physical cash counted, and generate your Shift Summary report.
2

Sales, Invoices, Customer Records & Loans

Processing checkout, receipts, customer profiles, and credit sales

The Point of Sale (POS) register enables rapid item scanning, custom thermal receipt formats, and credit sales tracking.

Recording Sales

Select products or scan barcodes. Choose payment method (Cash, Mobile Money, Card, Split). Click Complete Sale to print receipts or download PDF invoices.

Customer Credit & Loans

Register customer accounts under Customers. Record credit sales under Loans & Credit to track partial payments, interest, and due dates.

3

Operational Constraints & Administrative Lockouts

Understanding policy restrictions set by your Business Owner (BSO)

Your access permissions are configured dynamically by your Business Owner (BSO).

System Policy Lockouts:

  • Cash Drawer Requirement: If required by your BSO, sales checkout is locked until a starting shift float is registered.
  • Inventory Editing: Stock quantity adjustments or price overrides are disabled if locked by your BSO.
  • Security Violation Warnings: Attempting to force unauthorized actions will trigger a lockout alert and report directly to the BSO Security Audit Manager.
4

Offline Synchronization & Data Management

Uninterrupted sales operations during internet outages

BMSTz features an automatic offline engine designed for continuous operations during internet disconnections.

How Offline Sync Works:

If internet drops during shift hours, BMSTz automatically queues sales and receipts in local browser storage. When internet connection returns, the system silently syncs queued transactions to the cloud database.
