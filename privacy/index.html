Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At BMSTz ("we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal and business data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Business Management System.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and business details.

Business Data: We securely store data you input into the system, including inventory, sales records, customer details, and branch information.

Payment Data: Payments are processed securely via Snippe. We do not store full credit card or mobile money PINs on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to provide, maintain, and improve the BMSTz platform. We may use your email to send essential account notifications, receipts, or customer support responses.

3. Data Security & Storage

Your business data is hosted on highly secure cloud infrastructure (Supabase/AWS) with strict access controls. Branch employees can only access the data permitted by their assigned role (e.g., Cashier vs. Manager).

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third parties. We may share limited data with trusted infrastructure partners (e.g., Resend for emails, Snippe for payments) strictly for providing our service.

5. Offline Functionality

Our app utilizes local browser storage (IndexedDB and Service Workers) to temporarily queue sales when your device is offline. This data is synced to our secure servers once an internet connection is restored.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: bmstzsupport@gmail.com
